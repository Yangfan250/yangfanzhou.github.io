# Overview

During my summer research experience, I worked with single-walled carbon nanotubes (SWCNTs) and participated in several aspects of their production, purification, and data analysis.


## Data Analysis and Purification

One of my main responsibilities was analyzing experimental data from SWCNT samples before and after acid washing. I processed a large amount of purity data and generated plots to compare the results under different experimental conditions.

By comparing the data at different acid-washing temperatures, I helped identify the temperature associated with the strongest improvement in SWCNT purity.


## SWCNT Production

In addition to data analysis, I participated in parts of the SWCNT production and processing workflow. This experience gave me a better understanding of how experimental materials are produced, processed, and evaluated.


## Chemical Vapor Deposition Equipment

I also assisted with the construction and assembly of chemical vapor deposition (CVD) equipment used in SWCNT production.


## Commercial Product Comparison

Another part of my work involved comparing the purity data of our SWCNT products with commercially available SWCNT products. This allowed us to evaluate differences in material purity and overall performance.


## Technical Writing and Product Communication

I contributed to writing external promotional and informational articles about SWCNT products. These articles introduced the characteristics, production, and potential applications of single-walled carbon nanotubes to external audiences.
